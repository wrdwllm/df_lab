#### 3D scans

Data that documents the structure of physical reality, e.g., LIDAR, STL, point clouds.

Sources include:
- Bespoke scans
- [[Platforms#Digital Twin Victoria]]

#### Geospatial data

Data that describes and position objects in physical space in relation to a coordinate system, e.g., cadastral maps, location of trees, transit networks, animal sightings, etc.

Sources include:
- OpenStreetMap
- [[Platforms#Digital Twin Victoria]]
- Australian Living Atlas

#### Sensor data

In situ measurements of values using sensors, e.g., noise levels, light levels, movement tracking.

Sources include:
- Arduino sensors
- Specialist equipment, e.g., light meters
- Consumer monitoring devices like cameras

#### Statistical metrics 

For example, synthetic estimates of biodiversity, average solar exposure. These estimates are typically projections for a specific geographical location, e.g., a local government area or Federal electorate. They can be extrapolated to predict an implied value for a position based on a local average, which is useful during design and planning.

Sources include:
- [[Platforms#Digital Twin Victoria]]
- AURIN
- [Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/)
