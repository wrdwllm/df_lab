## Overview
Project by: [Reef Design Lab](https://www.reefdesignlab.com), [Sydney Institute of Marine Science](https://sims.org.au/)

![[attachments/example_living-seawalls.jpg|500px]]

> Seawalls are an ever-present structure in our intertidal environments and are rarely designed in a way that provides shelter for native marine animals. Normally a seawall is completely flat and devoid of crevices minimising the potential for colonising organisms. The aim of the living seawalls project is to develop research-based techniques for designing marine infrastructure in the future. We explore how digital fabrication techniques such as 3D printing can be utilised to minimise cost and increase habitat complexity.


## Relevance

This project is not directly relevant for Fishermans Bend stakeholders, but the model is applicable to other [[tags/example/artificial-habitat|artificial habitats]].
