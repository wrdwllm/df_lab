All designs should take the needs of non-humans into account. For example, the [[Stakeholders/Superb fairy-wren|Superb fairy-wren]] may not inhabit an [[Examples/Artificial trees|artificial tree]] or [[tags/example/artificial-hollow|artificial hollow]] if it is not surrounded by dense vegetation. An intervention needs to be sited strategically to be effective.
## Attachments
Attach [[tags/example/artificial-habitat|artificial habitats]] to existing structures:
- [[Examples/Insect hotel|Bee hotels]] to building walls
- [[tags/example/artificial-hollow|Artificial hollows]] to columns, trees, and power poles
- [[tags/example/bioreceptive-surface|Bioreceptive surfaces]] to building walls and infrastructure
## New habitats
Design novel habitat analogues that can provide new habitats:
- [[Examples/Artificial trees|Artificial trees]] that integrate [[tags/example/artificial-hollow|artificial hollows]]
- Walls, columns, and infrastructure with [[tags/example/bioreceptive-surface|bioreceptive surfaces]] 
## Connectivity
Design connections that link new habitats, existing habitats, and proposed habitats:
- Attach [[Examples/Hume Highway wildlife crossings|wild-life bridges]] or [[Opportunities/Animal underpasses|animal underpasses]] to infrastructure, columns, power poles, and buildings
## Integrations
Integrate the above solutions with:
- Sensors that gather [[Resources/Data|more-than-human data]]
- [[Opportunities/Eco-street lighting|Eco-street lighting]]
- Planting and vegetation strategies
