### Biodiversity

#### [Biodiversity Sensitive Urban Design](https://icon-science.org/biodiversity-sensitive-urban-design/) (BSUD)

BSUD is a framework for urban development that aims to improve outcomes for nature and humans in cities.

There are five principles:

> 1. Maintain or create habitat for target species
> 2. Facilitate dispersal
> 3. Minimise disturbance and other threats
> 4. Facilitate natural processes (such as pollination)
> 5. Facilitate positive human-nature interactions
