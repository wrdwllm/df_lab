This section contains personas for non-human stakeholders in Fishermans Bend. These stakeholders are adapted from the seven target species identified in the [[../Resources/Official Documents#Biodiversity Report|Biodiversity Report]] prepared by ICON Science for the Fishermans Bend Urban Ecology Strategy.

### Personas

Personas describe the movements, relationships, needs, and habitat preferences of stakeholders to help designers make informed decisions.
