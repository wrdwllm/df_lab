## Example Project
Project by: Alexander Holland, Stanislav Roudavski, Phillip Gibbons.

![[../attachments/solution-artificial-tree.avif]]

> Grassy box gum woodlands once covered millions of square kilometres in southeastern Australia, but today [less than 5% remains](https://www.nespthreatenedspecies.edu.au/projects/conservation-of-box-gum-grassy-woodlands-and-the-threatened-species-within-them). The loss of [large old trees](https://theconversation.com/the-end-of-big-trees-11217) has been a crisis for the many species of birds and other animals that depend on them for habitat.
>
> Replacing this habitat is not easy. There is no quick way to create a centuries-old tree.
>
> One thing we can do is make artificial structures that mimic the features of large old trees in degraded environments where trees cannot live or are too young and small.

[The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/what-makes-a-good-tree-we-used-ai-to-ask-birds-233281)

This project is broadly relevant for [[tags/stakeholder/bird|birds]], [[tags/stakeholder/insect|insects]] and [[tags/stakeholder/plant|plants]], but specific requirements depend on the species and ecosystem.
## Fishermans Bend

The [[../Resources/Fishermans Bend#Fishermans Bend Urban Ecology Strategy Biodiversity Report (2019)|Biodiversity Report]] recommends:

- planting of new canopy trees and preservation of "existing hollow bearing trees."[^1] 
- "novel habitat analogues" including "artificial cavities in buildings".[^2]
### Design opportunities

This typology could provide new hollows in combination with [[tags/solution/type/artificial-habitat|artificial habitats]] for [[tags/stakeholder/bird|birds]] like [[Nest Boxes for Swallows]] or [[Prosthetic nests for the Powerful Owl]].

Designs for artificial trees could integrate [[Eco-street lighting]].

Consider in tandem with [[../Design considerations|design considerations]] and needs of individual stakeholders.

The report also recommends that:

- "All canopy trees should be predominantly native providing a mixture of vegetation structures (low & high canopy) and services (flowers, hollows, dense foliage)."[^1]

Artificial trees cannot provide the same canopy coverage and habitat complexity as natural trees.
