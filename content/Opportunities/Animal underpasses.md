[[tags/example/connectivity|Habitat connectivity]] is important in urban environments. Traffic, pollution, heat, and lack of food and shelter prevent non-humans from flourishing. Connecting green spaces and habitats via protected corridors improves interconnectivity.

![[Sites/Go Kart site#Future|Go Kart site]]

The report recommends these specifications:

>- Gridded underpass to allow light penetration.
>- Should have a permeable base to allow water drainage and vegetation growth. Ideally be kept moist or wet.
>- Should also include a lipped drift fence/funnel for directing animal movement and preventing casualties.
>- Open onto understorey garden beds in the connected parks.

## Examples

Traditional animal underpasses have been developed by ecologists and implemented by landscape designers. They are successful and effective, but could their design be more ambitious?

While not an underpass, a parallel concept is wild-life bridges that run over traffic corridors. These are not appropriate for ground-dwelling stakeholders, but they could benefit non-priority species like possums.
