The [[../Resources/Fishermans Bend#Fishermans Bend Urban Ecology Strategy Biodiversity Report (2019)|Biodiversity Report]] recommends:

>- Inclusion of artificial cavities in buildings.[^1]
## Opportunities

- Attach [[../Examples/Bee hotel|bee hotels]] and [[tags/example/nest|nests]] to buildings, [[../Typologies/Columns|columns]], [[../Typologies/Power poles|power poles]], or [[../Typologies/Trees|trees]]
- Pair with [[Artificial trees|artificial trees]] to provide nesting hollows or temporary perches for [[../Stakeholders/Superb fairywren|Superb fairywren]] and other [[tags/stakeholder/bird|birds]]
	- Position near planters with ground cover
- Situate [[../Examples/Artificial rocks|artificial rocks]] with hollows for [[../Stakeholders/Blue-tongue Lizard|Blue-tongue Lizard]]

- Use [[Eco-street lighting|eco-street lighting]] to respond to requirements of nocturnal hollow-dwellers
